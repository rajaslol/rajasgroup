About

Partners in possibility

RajasGroup is an early stage venture investment firm built on a simple belief: the most consequential companies are born from ideas that seem impossible until they are not.

We seek out founders who are reimagining industries from first principles and back them before the conventional wisdom catches up. Our involvement does not end at the term sheet. We become long term thinking partners, committed to each company's growth through every phase.

We bring capital, networks, and decades of cross sector experience to every partnership, with the patience to let bold visions unfold at the right pace.
